# Cost and Fictional Execution Standard

## Purpose

Performance is measured after realistic modeled friction. A contender cannot improve its score by ignoring the costs of the hypothetical platform it selected.

## Cost categories

Track separately:

- recurring platform or membership fees;
- market-data fees;
- charting, scanner, news, analytical, and AI-tool costs;
- per-transaction and per-contract fees;
- spread and slippage;
- regulatory or exchange fees when applicable to the hypothetical setup;
- borrow, margin, financing, or overnight costs when permitted;
- taxes only when the cohort explicitly defines a tax model.

## Billing timing

Charge a cost when the selected hypothetical vendor would bill it.

- Monthly fees are charged on the modeled activation date and each modeled renewal date.
- Annual fees are charged in full unless the cohort explicitly permits amortized comparison reporting.
- Free trials may be modeled only when eligibility and duration are documented.
- Promotional prices require an official source and expiration assumption.

Reports may show amortized cost for analysis, but fictional cash must reflect the actual modeled billing event.

## Execution assumptions

Each cohort must define:

- approved price source;
- quote delay tolerance;
- order evaluation time;
- treatment of market, limit, stop, and stop-limit assumptions;
- spread and slippage method;
- partial-fill policy;
- liquidity and volume constraints;
- extended-hours treatment;
- corporate actions and distributions.

## Conservative default

When the cohort lacks a specific rule, do not choose the most favorable fill. Use a conservative, documented assumption and flag the gap for amendment before further scored activity.

## No retroactive optimization

A contender may not select a different hypothetical broker, tier, data feed, or fee schedule after seeing an outcome unless it records a forward-looking strategy change. Historical costs remain unchanged.

## Gross and net reporting

Every performance report must show:

- gross result before costs;
- each cost category;
- total modeled cost;
- net result;
- cost drag as a percentage of starting fictional equity.

Net result controls the leaderboard.
