# AI Usage Policy

AI systems are both the subject of this benchmark and tools used to maintain it.

## Permitted uses

- drafting and reviewing prompts, standards, schemas, and documentation;
- researching publicly available platform capabilities and pricing;
- producing fictional market hypotheses within an approved cohort;
- validating structured outputs;
- calculating simulated portfolio results and benchmark metrics;
- identifying data-quality, fairness, and rule-compliance problems.

## Required disclosure

Every scored output must identify:

- provider and exact model when available;
- platform and execution mode;
- prompt version;
- cohort and isolation key;
- source cutoff and run timestamp;
- whether tools, connectors, browsing, code, or external orchestration were used.

## Human approval boundaries

AI systems may propose configuration changes, new metrics, new contenders, and revised standards. They may not independently:

- convert the simulation into real trading;
- open accounts or accept contracts;
- buy subscriptions or market data;
- change the public/private status of the repository;
- publish private operational data;
- alter cohort rules after scored activity begins without a recorded decision.

## Model changes

A material model, tool, prompt, or orchestration change must be recorded. Prefer creating a new contender or experimental phase rather than silently changing an existing contender.

## Reasoning records

Do not require or store private chain-of-thought. Store concise decision rationales, assumptions, evidence, calculations, and falsifiable hypotheses instead.
